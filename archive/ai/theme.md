# Theme

- **Name:** Tahleel (تحليل, "analysis"). Not cleared for public use.
- **Look:** ink on paper, editorial, bento cards. No accent colour.

## Colour

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| paper | `#F6F5F1` | Background |
| card | `#FFFFFF` | Cards |
| ink | `#18181B` | Text, headings, the Now outline |
| mute | `#6B6B70` | Labels and captions |
| line | `#DCDAD2` | Borders |
| fill | `#ECEAE3` | Hover, tags |
| later | `#EFEEE9` | Greyed-out stages |

## Type

- Headings: Fraunces, 400.
- Interface: Inter, 15px body.
- Arabic: system Arabic font stack, `dir="rtl"`.

## Status treatment

- **Now:** white card, ink outline.
- **Next:** white card, slight dim.
- **Later:** grey card, dimmed text and icons.
- **Gated:** paper card, dashed border, most dimmed.

## Motion

- Reveal on scroll: fade and rise 8px, once.
- Hover lift 2px on Now and Next only.
- Reduced motion turns all of it off.

# User flow (one workflow)

Chat-style. One path from objective to plan. Nothing else in the first version.

## Flow

1. **Home (New).** One question: "What is your research objective?" Enter starts. Sample objectives can be clicked.
2. **Objective.** The researcher types one sentence. It shows as their message.
3. **Four confirmations.** Tahleel proposes a default for each block. Each block has Edit (change it) and Next (accept it).
   - **Target population.** Default generated from the objective.
   - **Sampling and design.** Design (survey, pre/post, experiment) and sampling approach.
   - **Constructs and questions.** Constructs with sub-questions. Edit names and questions, add or remove constructs.
   - **Questionnaire quality.** Checks on every question, with suggested rewrites. Apply a rewrite or keep the question, then Generate plan.
4. **Plan.** Two panels:
   - **Main:** the complete survey plan in sections (objective, population, design, constructs and questions, quality, ethics).
   - **Right:** chat to edit the plan ("add a construct on trust", "switch to Arabic").
5. **Actions on the plan.**
   - Ethics submission (dummy for now).
   - Generate form: Tahleel form (default), Google Forms or Microsoft Forms (dummy for now).
   - Language switch: English or Arabic headings.
   - Quality score.
6. **Recent.** The sidebar shows only recent plans. Click one to reopen it.

## Rules

- One workflow only. No library, folders or other menus in this version.
- Every default is marked as proposed. Nothing is final until the researcher confirms it.
- Estimates are illustrative until a statistician reviews them.
